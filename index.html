2 · How it plays

    
    

      
    
        
  1. Hold Space (or the big button) to code. The longer you go uninterrupted, the more Flow you build. At DEEP WORK you work 3× faster.
    2. 
        
  2. Notifications keep arriving. Respond and you're pulled away for a while. Ignore and it's gone, but ignoring something genuinely urgent costs Reputation.
    3. 
        
  3. Some messages are traps. Anything quick, small or just 2 mins never is.
    4. 
        
  4. ⏳ A run of right calls buys time. Twelve right calls in a row — Respond to what's
        urgent, tap Ignore on what isn't — and you bank 5 seconds. They come off your next
        interruptions automatically: when you're on top of your inbox you get off calls faster. A trap
        taken, an emergency missed, a polite no or a reply to small talk all end the run. A message you
        simply let run out counts for nothing either way — unless it was urgent, which ends it.
    5. 
        
  5. 🙅 Say no when you genuinely can't tell. It costs a few points of Reputation every time,
        whatever the message turns out to be — far less than ignoring a real emergency, and far more than
        reading it properly. It's for the messages you can't read in time, not for all of them.
    6. 
        
  6. 🎧 Headphones, once per round: 10 seconds with no non-urgent pings.
    7. 
        
  7. 🤝 Colleague favours: reply to small talk from a colleague (a person, not a bot, group chat or family) and they owe you one, up to 3. Tap 🤝 on any message to pass it to them, even while you're stuck on a call. They'll handle a real emergency for you; on anything else the favour is wasted.
    8. 
      

      
Goal: get the feature shipped (100%) with your reputation intact.
